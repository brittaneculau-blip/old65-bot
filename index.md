# OLD65 — Open Horizon RP

OLD65 is the Discord bot used by Open Horizon RP through txAdmin.

## Terms of Use

Last updated: 4 November 2026

### Purpose
OLD65 supports the connection between our Discord community and our RedM server. It checks Discord server membership when membership-based access control is enabled. It may also display server status and send server announcements.

### Community Rules
Players must follow the Open Horizon RP rules and the applicable Discord and Cfx.re terms. Discord membership does not override a server ban or other access restriction.

### Acceptable Use
Do not abuse the bot, attempt to bypass access checks, impersonate staff, or interfere with its operation.

### Availability
The bot may be unavailable during maintenance or technical problems. If you believe access was refused incorrectly, contact the team for review.

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

### Who is responsible?

This notice covers OLD65 and the associated txAdmin access checks and administration records. It does not cover every separate gameplay script on the RedM server.

### What data is processed?
For Discord membership checks, the system processes your Discord user ID, the community’s Discord server ID, and your membership status. Role information is also processed if role-based access is enabled.

The associated txAdmin player database stores player names, platform identifiers, server-specific hardware tokens, connection timestamps, and accumulated playtime. Administrative records may include warnings, bans, their reasons, and related staff actions. Technical logs can contain connection information and errors.

Data comes from Discord, the Cfx.re/RedM connection, your use of the server, and authorised staff actions.

### Why is this information used?
Information is used to check access eligibility, operate the server, enforce community rules, investigate technical problems, prevent abuse, and handle support requests.

For these purposes, the intended legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interests in operating a secure, moderated roleplay community. Processing must remain necessary and proportionate to those purposes.

### How does the access check work?
When Discord membership is required, txAdmin checks whether the Discord identifier supplied with your connection belongs to a member of our Discord server. A missing identifier or unsuccessful membership check can prevent access.

If you believe a refusal is incorrect, contact us for a manual review. Discord membership does not override an existing server ban.

### Who can access the information?
Access is limited to authorised staff and service providers where necessary to operate and host the system. Our game server is hosted through Nitrado. Discord processes requests needed for the membership check.

Discord, Cfx.re/Rockstar, Nitrado, and GitHub also process information under their own applicable privacy notices. This information page is hosted through GitHub Pages.

### How long is information kept?
Retention depends on its purpose. Player records support ongoing account recognition and administration. Technical logs support troubleshooting and security investigations. Moderation records may need to remain available while a restriction is active or an appeal or abuse investigation is unresolved.

Information should be deleted or anonymised when it is no longer necessary, unless a legal obligation or the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims requires further retention. Leaving Discord does not automatically delete txAdmin records.

### Your rights

Contact absinth2005@gmx.eu to exercise your rights. We may request proportionate information to verify that a request concerns your account. Never send passwords or authentication tokens.
